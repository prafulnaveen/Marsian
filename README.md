# Architectural Katas 2026: AI Assisted Software Architecture


# BACKGROUND
The estates are large and sprawling, and are in need of some help to make them profitable The family’s previous business of making unfortunately highly explosive garden gnomes has proved to no longer be viable
 
Owner is looking to bring some digital solutions to help her monetize parts of the estate.
 
There is an extensive, historically important collection of 18th century amusement park rides on the estates too. The rides have recently passed safety inspections after all the asbestos, broken glass and garden gnomes were removed
 
The previously private exotic and poisonous animal collection is also going to be opened to the public There is a mix of aquatic and land-based animals
 
The Estates get on average get 5000 visitors a day, with expectation (and hope!) that this will grow to at least 15000 within three years, or else the family may be forced to sell their carnivorous plant collection There are 40 rides in the amusement park area, and over 200 animals in exotic animal collection split across 55 different displays and enclosures 
 
# WHAT WE NEED
We need a system that provides the ability for people to buy tickets, including family passes to access the estates We want to understand how popular different parts of the park are, so we can understand where we need to improve things The exotic animal collection also requires careful monitoring - we want ways of tracking animal health, how much/well they are eating, and in the case of the jumping pirana collection, we also need to check population levels We also need to work out how to grow the number of visitors, but also make the estates more profitable, or it’s back to the garden gnome business
 
# Technical Context/Constraints 
Wifi coverage on the park is patchy You can make use of cloud services, but you need to have some ways of getting information from the estate to the cloud Assume there is a budget for MQTT-capable hardware devices which can be installed throughout the park 
 
# Biggest business challenges 
We have no real idea of what parts of the estates are most popular, so it’s difficult to know where to invest & deploy staff Looking after the animals is costly, even more so if they get sick! So we want healthy and happy animals We want to get more returning visitors too, but aren’t sure how
