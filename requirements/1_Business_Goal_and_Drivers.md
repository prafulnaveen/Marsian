# Business Goals & Drivers

> Note: This document explains how _business drivers_ (factors, resources, and processes that have a major impact on a business's performance and success) affect _business goals_ (specific, measurable, and time-bound objectives that a business aims to achieve) which are implemented through the project _solutions_:
> **Business Drivers → Business Goals → Capabilities / Solutions**


## Business Drivers

-  **Profitability**: To make the large, sprawling estate profitable and financially viable.
- **Monetization**: Implement digital solutions to monetize the estate's unique assets, including a historic amusement park and an exotic animal collection.
- **Visitor Growth**: Increase the average number of daily visitors from the current 5,000 to at least 15,000 within a three-year timeframe.
- **Visitor retention**: Vistor retention is requireed indicating the need for the loyality programs.
- **Business Viability**: Find a new, sustainable business model as the previous venture (explosive garden gnomes) is no longer viable.
- **Data-Driven Decisions**: Use data on park popularity to make informed decisions about investments, staffing, and improvements.
- **Financial Stability**: Generate enough profit to avoid selling off other family assets, such as the carnivorous plant collection.
- **Estate Heritage Asset**: Rides in estate are historically important implying presevation. Complaince on how they can be comercialized will be required.
